How the scores work, and what was actually reviewed

Priority = 30% JD relevance + 25% practical artifact potential + 20% technical depth + 15% currency/durability + 10% study efficiency. Each dimension is scored 1–5, then converted to 10. Highest priority is not a claim of perfection.
